Unpacked Traps (2)

FLAG: RED

"...deposit of $50 is required to set up your secure workstation..."

Mechanism: Advance Fee Fraud

Scammers invent fake "setup fees" or "equipment costs". Legitimate employers never charge candidates to start working.
